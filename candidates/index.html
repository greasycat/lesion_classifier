What am I looking at?
  • A1 is the frozen M-SYNTH-trained lesion heatmap (2.7 mm cells). Its top-20 peaks, at least 10 mm apart, are the candidates. A candidate hits when its centre is within 4 mm of the annotated mass.
  • Concept model: a ResNet-50 on a 40 mm crop around each candidate, trained on CBIS Mass-Training. It gives mass present, shape, margin and size. Malignancy comes from a logistic head on those predicted concepts.
  • Mammo-FM probe: a logistic probe (mass present) on frozen Mammo-FM embeddings pooled over 20 and 40 mm boxes, fitted on CBIS Mass-Training. The same probe, applied at every cell of Mammo-FM's feature map, gives the Mammo-FM heatmap.
  • Boxes vs heatmap: re-ranking only reorders the places the box source proposed. With A1's candidates on the Mammo-FM map, the boxes sit where A1 peaked, which is often not where Mammo-FM is hot: in this sample Mammo-FM's hottest spot is more than 10 mm from every A1 candidate in about 44 % of images. By default the boxes are Mammo-FM's own map peaks (same 10 mm separation rule), so boxes and heatmap agree; switch Boxes to A1's candidates to see A1 re-ranked. The concept model reads those peaks too, and A1's score there is its cell value. The fusion weights were chosen on A1's candidates, so applying them to Mammo-FM peaks is an extrapolation.
  • Fusions add standardised scores, z/sd + a·other/sd. The weights were chosen on CBIS validation patients and then fixed. CBIS Mass-Test is in-site for everything fitted on CBIS. TOMPEI and DMID are external.
  • Calibration gives the pixel lesion probability at a declared image prevalence. The Mammo-FM calibrators were fitted on the INbreast cal half (outline labels) or on the VinDr training split (box ellipses, about 1.7× more lesion pixels). Use INbreast's for outline-scored sets like these. A1 is shown as its raw score, sigmoid(z), on the relative scale. Its M-SYNTH calibrator does not transfer: on real images it flattens the map and runs about 20× too cold (INbreast pixel O/E 24).
  • Probabilities shown for single candidates (concept, probe) are raw model outputs at the training base rate, not calibrated.
